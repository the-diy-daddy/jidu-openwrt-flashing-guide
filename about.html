About This Resource

The OpenWrt Flashing Guide & Compatibility Master-Chart is an independent, community-driven technical documentation project dedicated to embedded networking hardware, Linux-based router firmware, and open-source network security.

Our Mission

Many second-hand and legacy consumer routers contain capable MIPS and ARM networking hardware that becomes e-waste once official vendor updates end. Our goal is to document hardware revisions, UART pinouts, bootloader behaviors, and open-source OpenWrt installation workflows so hobbyists, students, and network engineers can safely repurpose owned hardware.

What You Will Find Here

  • Compatibility Matrices: Verified mappings between router hardware families (6101, 6j01, and 6j11) and supported stock firmware builds.
  • Step-by-Step Flashing Documentation: Written guides covering SSH access, sysupgrade procedures, and 3.3V serial UART recovery.
  • Post-Flash Networking Guides: Practical instructions for configuring OpenWrt operating modes, AdGuardHome DNS filtering, and WireGuard VPN tunnels.

Educational & Research Scope

All tutorials, compatibility matrices, and video walkthroughs published on this site are intended strictly for educational research and personal hardware ownership. We do not sell firmware, host proprietary vendor binaries, or encourage tampering with ISP-owned equipment under active service contracts.
